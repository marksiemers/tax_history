Reading the table. “Bottom” and “Top” are the lowest and highest statutory marginal rates in force that year for the chosen filing status; “Top starts” is the income at which the top rate begins, shown both in the dollars of the day and restated in 2026 dollars using the consumer price index. Blank tax years (1873–1912) reflect the lapse of the Civil War income tax and the pre-16th-Amendment period.

Sources. Statutory rates and brackets from the historical income-tax tables (Tax Foundation / IRS). Price levels from the Federal Reserve Bank of Minneapolis consumer price index (1913–present, with pre-1913 estimates). Caveat: CPI conversions measure price levels only — not relative incomes or living standards — and pre-1913 figures are historical reconstructions, so treat the deep-past dollar equivalents as illustrative.
